# Asset Journal

## Purpose
Track every external or generated asset used in published FUN content.

## Rules
- Every external asset must have a confirmed commercial-use basis before publication.
- Record source URL/provider, asset type, license/plan, date checked and where it is used.
- Do not treat a source video as an asset merely because it is used as trend inspiration.
- AI-generated assets are logged with the generation tool and relevant commercial-use terms.
- If rights are unclear, do not publish the asset.
