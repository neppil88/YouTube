## 2026-10-04 — First niche analysis

### Goal
Найти нишу с лучшим сочетанием:
- простоты производства;
- возможности работать без лица;
- совместимости с AI-assisted production;
- copyright/policy safety;
- спроса;
- дохода с long-form;
- возможности использовать Shorts как источник трафика;
- дополнительных доходов (affiliate/sponsors);
- возможности масштабировать производство без превращения канала в mass-produced/inauthentic content.

### Important YouTube policy findings
1. YouTube's monetization policy says original/authentic content is required and clarifies that repetitive or mass-produced "inauthentic content" is ineligible for monetization. Reused content without significant original commentary/modification/value can also make a channel ineligible.
2. YouTube says monetizable videos/Shorts must be original/non-repetitious and creators must have the necessary commercial rights to visual/audio elements.
3. YouTube requires disclosure when AI meaningfully alters or generates photorealistic content. AI used for scripts, outlines, thumbnails, captions, idea generation and similar production assistance generally does not require disclosure. Disclosure itself does not reduce monetization eligibility.
4. YouTube YPP full ad-revenue eligibility currently requires either 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days. Shorts-feed watch hours do not count toward the 4,000-hour threshold.
5. YouTube advertiser-friendly rules apply to the whole video package: video, Short/live content, title, thumbnail, description and tags.

### Why finance is NOT our first choice
Finance has the highest advertiser value in many benchmark datasets, but it is not the easiest/safest starting point. It requires more factual accuracy and trust, and YouTube explicitly warns against AI personas presenting themselves as human experts giving financial guidance. That creates unnecessary policy and reputational risk for our initial AI-assisted channel.

### Recommended niche
## Winner: AI tools + software workflows + practical automation

The proposed channel should NOT be a generic "AI news" channel. The stronger concept is practical, searchable, demonstrable content such as:
- "How to use X to do Y"
- "Best AI tool for Z"
- "I tested X vs Y for a real task"
- "Automate this boring task with X"
- "3 workflows that save 2 hours"
- "X tutorial for beginners"
- comparisons and experiments based on actual hands-on testing.

### Why this wins
1. We can make the core visual material ourselves by recording the software/tool interfaces and our own workflows.
2. We do not need a face or expensive camera.
3. A video can be genuinely original because the value comes from our testing, screen recordings, explanation, examples, comparisons and conclusions.
4. The same research can produce one long-form video plus several Shorts without relying on third-party clips.
5. Software and AI products can support affiliate and sponsorship revenue in addition to YouTube ads.
6. It is easier to create a repeatable workflow while still making each video materially different.
7. The niche has strong commercial intent: viewers are often deciding whether to use/buy a tool.
8. It can target English-speaking viewers in US/Canada/UK/Australia, where advertiser value is generally stronger than many lower-CPM geographies.

### Main risks
- Very fast-changing information.
- Strong competition.
- Need to actually test tools rather than rewrite product pages.
- Some tools have restrictive commercial/affiliate terms; each monetization relationship must be checked.
- Screenshots/demos of third-party software are useful but we must avoid misrepresenting products or copying other creators' material.

### Recommended content model
**Long-form = core business**
- 6–12 minutes initially.
- Real demos, comparisons, experiments and tutorials.
- Build searchable evergreen videos.

**Shorts = acquisition/testing**
- 20–60 seconds initially.
- Strong hook + one useful result + CTA to the long-form video.
- Use Shorts to test topics; expand winning topics into long-form.

### Production difficulty estimate
For a simple tutorial:
- research/testing: 30–90 min
- outline/script: 20–40 min
- screen recording/assets: 20–45 min
- voiceover: 10–30 min
- edit: 30–90 min
- title/thumbnail/policy check: 20–30 min

Expected starting production time: roughly **2–5 hours per quality long-form video**, depending on complexity. This is a planning estimate, not an observed guarantee.

A Short derived from original material can often take roughly **20–60 minutes** after the underlying research is done, but we should not mass-produce near-identical Shorts.

### Revenue model
Priority order:
1. Long-form YouTube ads after YPP eligibility.
2. Affiliate commissions for relevant tools/software.
3. Sponsorships once audience quality is proven.
4. Potential digital products/templates/workflows later.
5. Shorts primarily as reach and discovery, not as the only income engine.

### Decision
**Do not choose the final channel name yet.**
Next we should validate the niche with a competitor/content-gap study and identify 20 concrete video ideas before branding.

### Sources / market benchmark context
Third-party 2026 benchmark articles consistently place finance/business above entertainment/gaming in advertiser value and identify AI/SaaS/software tutorials as strong faceless/AI-assisted categories. These are directional benchmarks, not guaranteed channel RPM:
- OutlierKit (2026): AI/software, finance, education and storytelling discussed as strong automation niches.
- Admetrics (2026): finance, business, tech and education benchmarked above gaming/entertainment.
- TubeAnalytics (2026): Canada benchmarks show finance and technology above education, entertainment and gaming.
These sources are used only for relative strategic comparison; actual revenue depends heavily on audience geography, format, seasonality and viewer intent.


## 2026-10-04 — Alternative strategy: original AI viral entertainment

Пользователь предложил модель: находить популярные тренды/сюжеты (особенно с котиками и другими животными) и создавать новые AI-видео на их основе.

### Assessment
Эта модель потенциально:
- проще и быстрее в производстве, чем AI/software tutorials;
- лучше подходит для Shorts/Reels и быстрого тестирования большого количества идей;
- потенциально имеет более высокий шанс отдельных вирусных всплесков;
- имеет существенно более низкий рекламный потенциал на один просмотр, чем коммерческие software/finance topics;
- сильнее зависит от постоянного поиска трендов;
- имеет повышенный риск монетизационного провала, если фактически повторяет чужие ролики, шаблоны или производит массовый однотипный AI-контент.

### Critical rule
НЕ делать:
- скачивать популярный Short/Reel/TikTok и просто менять лицо/фон/стиль через AI;
- делать почти одинаковые ролики, меняя только животное, текст или фон;
- брать чужой оригинальный audio/video и считать, что AI-editing автоматически делает его нашим;
- копировать узнаваемого персонажа или конкретную историю настолько близко, что результат является практически переделкой исходного произведения.

YouTube states that repetitive/mass-produced "inauthentic content" is not eligible for monetization, and reused content must have significant original commentary, substantive modifications, or educational/entertainment value. On 2026-10-01 YouTube also announced that Shorts recommendations will further prioritize original content and reduce reach for reuploads without meaningful original contribution.

### Better model
Use the trend only as **inspiration**:
**Trend discovery → identify the underlying hook → write a new story/joke → generate original visuals/audio → edit with our own pacing/narration → publish.**

Example:
Trend: cute cat rescue videos are going viral.
Wrong: download one viral cat rescue and remake the exact sequence with AI.
Right: use the general "cat rescue" hook and create a completely new fictional 30-second story with our own cat character, new setting, new conflict, new ending, and original narration/audio.

### Best format for this idea
**Original AI animal entertainment universe**
- recurring original characters;
- short fictional stories;
- comedy / surprise / emotional endings;
- current meme/trend hooks without copying the source execution;
- 20–60 second Shorts as primary format;
- occasional 3–8 minute compilations or expanded stories only when they provide genuinely new value.

### Monetization assessment
This is likely easier for reach than AI/software tutorials, but weaker as a direct advertising business. The business model should therefore be:
1. Shorts audience growth;
2. YPP Shorts revenue when eligible;
3. long-form/compilations with original additional storytelling;
4. sponsorships/brand integrations later;
5. possible digital products/characters/merch only if a recognizable original IP develops.

### Recommendation
Keep two candidates alive:
- **A. AI tools + practical automation** — higher commercial value and better direct monetization;
- **B. Original AI animal/viral entertainment** — easier production and higher viral upside.

Before final channel branding, test both with a small controlled experiment and compare:
- production time per video;
- views;
- average percentage viewed / retention;
- subscribers gained per 1,000 views;
- repeat viewers;
- comments/shares;
- estimated revenue when data becomes available.
