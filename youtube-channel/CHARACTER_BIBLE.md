# CHARACTER BIBLE — FUN

## Primary character
**Name:** Milo

### Visual identity
- Species: domestic short-haired orange tabby cat.
- Fur: warm orange with distinct darker tabby stripes.
- Eyes: large amber-brown eyes with highly readable expressions.
- Build: small-to-medium, slightly rounded, agile.
- Distinctive accessory: dark teal fabric collar with a small round brass tag.
- No logos, branded clothing or recognizable copyrighted design elements.

### Personality
- Curious.
- Overconfident when trying a new job.
- Kind-hearted.
- Slightly dramatic reactions.
- Solves problems through improvisation rather than aggression.

### Performance rules
Milo should communicate primarily through facial expressions, body language and physical comedy.
Avoid human-like speech in every scene; silence or short narration makes the character more internationally readable.

### Visual consistency
Every prompt involving Milo should preserve:
- orange tabby fur pattern;
- amber-brown eyes;
- dark teal collar;
- small round brass tag;
- same approximate body proportions.

## Secondary character
**Name:** Pip
- Species: tiny brown-and-white mouse.
- Personality: clever, mischievous, cooperative.
- Used sparingly as a recurring surprise character.
- No recognizable existing character resemblance.

## World rules
- Modern everyday world presented with playful anthropomorphic animal behavior.
- Cinematic lighting and believable environments.
- Humor without cruelty or animal distress.
- No copied internet characters, memes as characters, celebrity likenesses, logos or branded environments.

## Prompt anchor
Use a stable character anchor in every generation:
"small orange tabby domestic shorthair cat, warm orange fur with clear darker tabby stripes, large amber-brown expressive eyes, dark teal fabric collar with a small round brass tag, consistent body proportions, cinematic stylized 3D animation, detailed fur, expressive face, physically believable lighting"
