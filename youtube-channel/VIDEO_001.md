# VIDEO 001 — THE CAT'S FIRST NIGHT SHIFT

## Status
Pilot MP4 created

## Goal
First public pilot for FUN. Test visual hook, retention, character appeal and production workflow.

## Format
- 9:16 vertical
- Target: 32–38 seconds
- English
- General audience

## Hook
A small orange tabby cat arrives for its first night shift at a tiny convenience store. The automatic doors open by themselves. An empty shopping cart slowly rolls inside.

## Story
Scene 1 — 0–3s
Milo walks into the store wearing his dark teal collar. Text: "First night shift."

Scene 2 — 3–7s
The doors open. Empty cart rolls in by itself. Milo freezes and stares.

Scene 3 — 7–13s
Milo tries to ignore it and starts stocking snacks. The cart bumps a display.

Scene 4 — 13–19s
Milo chases the rolling cart. It makes a tiny circle around an aisle and comes back.

Scene 5 — 19–25s
Milo climbs into the cart to stop it. The cart suddenly moves faster.

Scene 6 — 25–30s
Milo notices two tiny paws hidden behind a snack box in the cart.

Scene 7 — 30–35s
Pip the mouse pops up and proudly presses a tiny button.

Scene 8 — 35–38s
Milo looks annoyed, then smiles. Final shot: Milo and Pip working together behind the counter.

## On-screen text
